About Gen22

More than a gym. A generation.

Gen22 was founded on a simple idea: fitness should be welcoming, results-driven and fun, for every age and every level.

Our story

Built for the next generation

Gen22 opened its doors in 2022 with one goal: create a gym where a first-timer and a competitive athlete feel equally at home. The name says it all: a new generation of fitness, built around coaching, community and results you can measure.

Today we're home to more than 1,200 members, 12 certified coaches and 40+ weekly classes. But we still greet every member by name.
