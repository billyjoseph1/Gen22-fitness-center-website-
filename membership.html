FAQ

Common questions

Is there a joining fee?

No. You only pay for your plan. Your first fitness assessment is free.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes. Freeze for up to 2 months per year for travel, illness or injury at no cost.

How do I cancel?

Monthly plans can be cancelled any time with 7 days' notice. Annual plans are paid upfront and non-refundable, but can be transferred to a friend.

How can I pay?

M-Pesa, card or bank transfer. Pay at the front desk or ask us for payment details on WhatsApp.

Do you offer student or corporate discounts?

Yes: 15% off for students with a valid ID and group rates for companies with 5+ employees. Ask at the front desk.

I'm a complete beginner. Is Gen22 right for me?

Absolutely. Most members started as beginners. Your free assessment and an intro session with a coach will get you started safely.

Not sure which plan?

Try us free for a day, then decide. A coach will help you pick.
